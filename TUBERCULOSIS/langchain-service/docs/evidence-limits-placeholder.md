## Validation

Model performance claims require evaluation on held-out data with patient-level and institution-level splits, calibrated thresholds chosen on development data only, and external validation. Sensitivity, specificity, AUC-ROC, AUC-PR and calibration should be reported with confidence intervals.

## Demo mode

The demonstration engine is not a validated model. Results produced in demo mode must not be interpreted clinically or reported as scientific performance.

## Known risks

Dataset shift between institutions, scanner differences and label noise can reduce sensitivity. False negatives require systematic analysis.
