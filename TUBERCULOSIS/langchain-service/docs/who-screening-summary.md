## Chest X-ray as a screening tool

Chest radiography is recommended as a screening and triage tool for pulmonary tuberculosis in people aged 15 years and older. A positive chest X-ray result is not a diagnosis: bacteriological confirmation is required.

## Computer-aided detection

Computer-aided detection software may be used instead of human readers to interpret digital chest radiographs for screening and triage of tuberculosis. Software output must be confirmed by a clinician and bacteriological testing.

## Confirmatory testing

Individuals with a positive screening result should receive a WHO-recommended rapid diagnostic test, such as a molecular assay, to confirm pulmonary tuberculosis.
